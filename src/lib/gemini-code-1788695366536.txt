### 1. CORE MISSION & PURPOSE OF FITGURA
Fitgura is a frictionless AI shopping aggregator for AliExpress. It eliminates manual sizing confusion and irrelevant feed items by:
- Automatically mapping the user's exact body/device profile to returned products.
- Enforcing absolute gender and category isolation across all feed tabs.
- Sorting products by Best Sellers (High Sales Volume) AND High Customer Ratings.
